Nitrite-specific notes:

- Nitrite reserves `_id` for its internal document identifier (NitriteId). Micronaut Data Nitrite stores your entity identifier in its mapped field, which defaults to `id`. A `Long` identity is also used as `_id`; other identity types use a separate internal document identifier.
- Nitrite also maintains internal fields like `_revision` and `_modified`. These are not mapped to your entity and are ignored during entity conversion.
